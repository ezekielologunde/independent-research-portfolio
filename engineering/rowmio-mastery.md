# Rowmio AI tutor and mastery diagnosis

Status: extension design scaffold. Existing local Rowmio directory found; application functionality, deployment and student usage have not been audited.

## Vertical slice

Choose one course unit and a small instructor-approved learning-objective map. A learner answers a diagnostic question, receives a cited hint tied to the suspected misconception, then attempts a different transfer question without tutor assistance. Show uncertainty rather than declaring mastery from one answer. Record content and rubric versions. Proposed modules: objective map, diagnostic service, approved-content retrieval, tutor session, independent assessment and instructor review.

Reuse the existing application after architecture inspection. Develop with synthetic learner records and openly licensed or owner-provided content. Keep instructor score changes under human control. A public demo must not expose actual student records.

## Evaluation plan

Compare static feedback or the existing experience with the tutor. First audit feedback correctness and rubric consistency using instructor-adjudicated examples. Report severe pedagogical errors, unsupported statements, diagnosis agreement, task completion and latency. Instructor-score agreement is not evidence of improved learning.

For learning outcomes, prospectively define independent transfer and delayed retention measures. If recruiting students, establish the applicable consent and institutional requirements before research collection. Record allocation, prior attainment, attrition and assistance conditions. Separate opt-in product usage from research participation and from grades.

Acceptance: one usable learning sequence, source-linked feedback, instructor correction path, versioned evaluation and clear separation of observed usage from learning effects. No student count or outcome is currently established.

Resume template, not a result: Shipped a tutor for [unit/course scope], used by [verified users], with [measured feedback quality or independent learning outcome] compared with [baseline].
